About this archive

A gazette is the instrument by which a government makes a decision public: appointments, land acquisitions, rules, tariffs, corrigenda. Each one is published by a government press on its own schedule, in its own series, and — for most of the country's history — on paper. This site collects those issues as they are published, and makes their text searchable.

How a gazette gets here

Issues are downloaded from the publishing site of each government press, along with the metadata that press records for them — date, gazette number, part, ministry, department, subject. The PDF is then converted into structured HTML, and it is that HTML which is displayed and indexed. Where the structural conversion loses something, a second, page-faithful rendering of the same PDF is offered alongside it as an alternate rendering; it is not indexed, and the structured text is the version to read and cite.

Searching

The search box queries the full text of every gazette held here, together with its subject, ministry and department. A hit in the title of an issue outranks one buried in its body.

  • "inter cadre transfer" — an exact phrase
  • railways OR highways — either term
  • acquisition -corrigendum — exclude a term

Very long issues are indexed by their opening section rather than in full; where that applies, the gazette's own page says so.

Citing an issue

Every gazette has a permanent address of the form /details/<identifier>/. That identifier is the same one used for the corresponding item at the Internet Archive, so a citation of either resolves in the other, and it does not change when this site is reorganised.

Status of these texts. The gazettes reproduced here are the public record as issued by the government presses that published them. This archive is an independent reference service and is not an official publication of any government. Conversion from print and PDF is automated and can introduce errors; where a matter turns on the exact wording, consult the gazette as issued.
