About this archive

Union and State Governments publish gazettes regularly on their websites informing citizens of the bills being considered, the laws that are being promulgated and the amendment to these laws. Governments also publish various rules, regulations, bye-laws, and statutory orders in the gazettes. Gazettes also contain important notifications like land acquisition, environment classification, town planning etc.

However, gazettes are published in PDFs many of which are scanned. PDFs are hard to read on small devices. Searching across gazette collection is also difficult. Secondaly, Indian Gazettes are published in regional laguages that use proprietory non-unicode fonts in the PDFs. They display correctly on the PDFs but when you extract the text, it is gibberish.

We convert all gazettes into HTML format with proper layout detection using our legallayout tool. It correctly recognizes paragraphs, blockquote, tables and images. It extracts images and makes them easily usable in the IIF manifest. We also convert all non-unicode proprietory fonts to unicode, using indic2unicode, making searching of gazettes in regional language possible.

How a gazette gets here

Gazettes are downloaded from the government websites along with the metadata like number, part, ministry, department, subject. The PDF is then converted into structured HTML, and it is that HTML which is displayed and indexed. To see how our structured HTML is different from the current PDF to HTML converters we show the pymupdf rendering of the same PDF as an alternate rendering; it is not indexed, and our structured HTML is the version to read and cite.

Searching

The search box queries the full text of every gazette held here, together with its subject, ministry and department. A hit in the title of an issue outranks matches in the rest of the gazettes. The query language can be:

  • "inter cadre transfer" — an exact phrase
  • railways OR highways — either term
  • acquisition -corrigendum — exclude a term

Citing an issue

Every gazette has a permanent address of the form /details/<identifier>/. That identifier is the same one used for the corresponding item at the Internet Archive, so a citation of either resolves in the other, and it does not change when this site is reorganised.

Status of these texts. The gazettes reproduced here are the public record as issued by the government presses that published them. This archive is an independent reference service and is not an official publication of any government. Conversion from print and PDF is automated and can introduce errors; where a matter turns on the exact wording, consult the gazette as issued.
