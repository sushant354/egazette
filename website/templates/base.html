Servants of Knowledge

Servants of Knowledge is a non-profit initiative based in Bengaluru dedicated to digitizing rare books, historical documents, and public data to make them freely accessible online. Our work has included digitisation of the entire National Law School of India University library, the Gandhi Bhavan Library, and many others. Some of the other organisations we have worked with include Roja Muthiah Library in Chennai, Vishwakonkani Centre in Mangaluru, Kannada University in Hampi, as well as Sochara Community Health Organisation, Ramkrishna Ashram, Azim Premji University, and Lalbagh Botanical Gardens.
